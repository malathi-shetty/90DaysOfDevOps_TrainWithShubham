# Day 27 – GitHub Profile Makeover

### Task 1: Audit Your Current GitHub Profile
Before making changes, assess where you stand:
1. Visit your own GitHub profile as if you were a stranger — what impression does it give?
2. Answer in your notes:
   - Is your profile picture professional?
        - My profile picture is not professional.
        - It does not create a strong first impression.
   - Is your bio filled in? Does it say what you do?
        - My bio was not properly filled in.
        - It did not clearly describe my role, experience, or DevOps learning journey.
   - Are your pinned repos relevant, or are they random forks?
        - The pinned repositories were mostly random or forked projects.
        - They did not effectively showcase my own work or learning progress.
   - Do your repos have descriptions, or are they blank?
       - Many repositories had missing or unclear descriptions.
       - It was difficult to understand the purpose of each project at a glance.
   - Would a recruiter understand what you've been working on?
        - My 13+ years of QA experience
        - My transition into DevOps
        - The type of work I have been doing
